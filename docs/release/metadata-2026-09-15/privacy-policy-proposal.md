## English — Privacy policy

OpenCCman converts text on your device using OpenCC. The app does not send the text you enter, import or convert to a conversion server. Preferences and daily conversion allowances are stored locally. Files you open or export are handled through the system file picker and the storage location you choose.

Purchases use Apple’s in-app purchase system and RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access and manage Pro entitlements. RevenueCat processes purchase records and an app user identifier; the current app uses RevenueCat’s anonymous identifier rather than asking you to create an OpenCCman account. Requests also include device, operating system and app version information needed by the purchase service. Purchase information also supports purchase analytics. See [RevenueCat’s privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

On Mac, global shortcuts use Accessibility permission and the clipboard to copy or replace selected text in other apps. You can manage this permission in System Settings. Opening support, privacy or recommended-app links takes you to external services. If you contact support by email, the message and information you choose to include are shared with the recipient.

Privacy questions: 531sunlight@gmail.com.

## 简体中文 — 隐私政策

OpenCCman 使用 OpenCC 在设备上转换文本。应用不会将您输入、导入或转换的正文发送到转换服务器。偏好设置和每日转换额度保存在本地。打开与导出文件通过系统文件选择器及您选择的存储位置处理。

购买功能使用 Apple App 内购买和 RevenueCat 验证购买、恢复权益及管理 Pro 权限。RevenueCat 处理购买记录和应用用户标识；当前应用使用 RevenueCat 的匿名标识，无需创建 OpenCCman 账号。请求还包含购买服务使用的设备、操作系统及应用版本信息。购买信息也用于购买分析。详见 [RevenueCat 隐私政策](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)。

在 Mac 上，全局快捷键使用辅助功能权限和剪贴板复制或替换其他应用中选中的文本；您可在系统设置中管理权限。打开支持、隐私政策或应用推荐链接会访问外部服务。若您通过电子邮件联系支持，收件人会收到邮件及您主动提供的信息。
